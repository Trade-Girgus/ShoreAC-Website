Member benefits

Membership is built around racing access, training support, and staying connected with the Shore A.C. community.

Race discounts

Members receive promo-code discounts for several Shore A.C. events, including major road races and Workout Wednesday.

Workouts

Adult track workouts are free for members, and youth athletes receive workout discounts.

Club communication

Members receive Shore A.C. newsletters, email updates, and access to club group chat communication.

USATF teams

Members can compete for Shore A.C. at USATF Grand Prix and championship events when they also complete USATF membership requirements.

Club gear

Membership includes access to team gear, and the official membership information lists a one-time Shore A.C. racing singlet benefit.

Community

Group runs, race teams, volunteer opportunities, and club events help members stay active beyond a single race day.

Membership with coaching

The coaching option is designed for athletes who want a focused plan for one or more races.

Select coaching membership

What is included

  • An individualized training plan, generally 8 to 10 weeks long.
  • The plan can target one race or a group of races.
  • At least one in-person session is included.
  • Additional coaching can be arranged separately when needed.

Not sure which membership fits?

Start with adult or youth membership if you mainly want club access and race discounts. Choose coaching if you want a race-specific training plan.
