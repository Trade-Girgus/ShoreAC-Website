Rah Mirth at Haworth: Shore AC Masters Men Sweep 5K

It has become a familiar sound at USATF-NJ races: Shore AC teams being announced as winners, and a rousing round of huzzahs in response!  This year's Haworth 5K was no exception.  Shore AC teams were pronounced champions in every contested age group, from 40+ through 70+.  With each announcement, the awards venue echoed with the proverbial "Rah!"

​Let's recount the exciting race by starting with our septuagenarians.  Human bullet train Jim Linn set the tenor, hurtling down the tracks -- er, road -- to reach the first mile marker in 6 minutes flat.  Jim's 72-year-old body couldn't sustain that pace, but he still finished with an astounding time of 21:09.  Former USATF Masters National Athlete of the Year Reno Stirrat proved that he can still fly, breaking the timing plane in 21:45.  Top-notch 74-year-old Peter Auteri following in 23:51 to complete team scoring.  Rock-solid insurance was added by potent runners Jack Frame (26:58), Bill Bosmann (27:00) and Dan Murphy (29:13).  So effective were they that, had Frame, Bosmann and Murphy been the three scorers, the team would still have won!  Bravo, gents!
September 27, 2025, Haworth, NJ: The victorious Shore AC men's 70+ team relishes the spotlight.  Left to right: Jack Frame, Jim Linn, Reno Stirrat, Bill Bosmann and Peter Auteri.  Not pictured: Dan Murphy.
Extra cheers erupted for the Shore AC 60+ crew, for they were comprised of not one, not two but three teams.  SAC ace Mike Salamone savored his return to full running strength by ripping an awesome 19:05 to lead the "A" team.  Rookie sensation Alberto Perez contributed a speedy 20:06 as #2 scorer.  When Linn zoomed through the balloon archway a minute later, it left no doubt: Shore AC's M60+ "A" team had conquered all comers with an average time of 20:07 per runner.

Among the M60+ competition, the team that came closest to Shore AC's M60+ "A" team was its "B" team.  With Stirrat leading the charge, doddering 69-year-old Scott Linnell shuffled across the finish line in 23:14 as second scorer, wondering how those halcyon days of effortless sub-7:00 pace could disappear so quickly.  Soon thereafter, Auteri slammed the door on "B" team scoring.  The team's 22:57 average bettered that of all other teams by more than 1 minute.

As for our Shore AC M60+ "C" team, it was a chance for the trio of Frame, Bosmann and Murphy to shine.  That they did, vanquishing the Fleet Feet/ Essex M60+ squad by more than 1 minute to secure sixth place.  Well done, lads!
Was there any drama in this race, you ask?  Yes, beforehand, at least in the younger age group team competitions.  Pre-race analysis projected absolute dogfights in the 50+ and 40+ team categories between Shore AC and Clifton Road Runners.  But, in the end, the SAC lads prevailed with solid margins of victory.

The Shore AC M50+ crew benefitted from terrific performances by several of its renowned quinquagenarians.  Seasoned harrier Ken Ginsburg spearheaded the attack, dashing through the sleepy town of Haworth at a 6-minute pace to finish in 18:37.  After Salamone sailed through the arch as second scorer, sophomore sizzler Joe Demetrick streaked down the final stretch in 19:12 as #3 man.  When Perez finished a minute later, there was one scoring position still open.  Yeoman runner John Delaney filled that position when he barreled across the timing strip in 20:43.  Fellow 50-59 harriers Bob Shields (21:18) and Chris Rinaldi (21:30), while adding backup insurance, left absolutely no doubt which 50+ team would prevail.  In the end, Shore AC's margin of victory totaled 7 minutes.  Outstanding accomplishment, men!

It was a similar story in the M40+ team tussle.  A pre-race edge to Cliton gave way to a comfortable win by Shore AC.  Our boys in white singlets were buoyed by superb performances by a couple of young bucks.  Freshman phenom Chris Peterson shook off the soreness from a marathon the week before to breeze across the finish line in 17:58.  The roaring crowd was just beginning to take their seats again when veteran SAC speedster Chris Moye came screeching down Haworth Avenue to wrap up his race in 18:33.  Ginsburg, Demetrick and Delaney polished off team scoring while Shields and Rinaldi added the always-vital insurance.  Once again, Shore AC had achieved victory with a 7-minute margin.  Bravo, boys!
