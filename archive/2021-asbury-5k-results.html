Asbury Alive with Runners at the Sheehan Classic 5K
For those yearning to "get back to normal", it was a heartening sight: 1,000 runners lined up for the start of the 2021 Asbury Park Sheehan Classic 5K race.  Shore Athletic Club boosters took special joy at the sight of so many Shore AC singlets, most gleaming white in the bright sunshine.  An astounding 57 SAC athletes graced the crowd.  Thanks to our quantity and quality, we reaped the most points (87) of any club in the USATF-NJ Long Distance Running Grand Prix team competition at this race.

For the second race in a row, we welcomed a host of new runners to the Shore AC LDR program.  Climbing aboard the Good Ship Shore AC in their first official USATF team race for us were these terrific athletes:
