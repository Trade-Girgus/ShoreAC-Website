MONMOUTH UNIVERSITY
400 Cedar Avenue, West Long Branch, N.J.
​
Our goal is to continue the great tradition of the NJ International Meet this summer. The Shore Athletic Club is excited to host the 39th edition of the meet in honor of Olympian and Club Founder, Elliott Denman. The meet will be held at Monmouth University’s new 8 lane track and promises to be one of the highlights of the 2026 track & field season bringing together the nation’s top athletes along with competition for masters and high school level competitors. The nation’s top club’s will be competing against each other vying top spots with cash prizes to be awarded to teams as well as individual cash prizes. Last year more than $8,200 were awarded to the top open and masters competitors. It’s a great time to visit the New Jersey shore and take advantage of one our nation’s premier vacation spots. Please stay tuned to the Shore AC’s website for the meet schedule and entry information and updates.
