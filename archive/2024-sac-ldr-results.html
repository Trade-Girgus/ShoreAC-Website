​​Shore AC Rocks in 2024

December 14, 2024, Tacoma, WA: Members of Shore Athletic Club's women's 60+ team proudly display the clubhouse banner that the club earned for placing first in the 2024 USATF Masters National Grand Prix in the women's 60+ team category.  Left to right: Leslie Nowicki, Deb Capko, Suzanne La Burt and Donna Grocki.
What a spectacular year!  Shore Athletic Club smashed the catapult and rang the bell in 2024.  At the USATF New Jersey Association level, for the second year in a row, we won every LDR Grand Prix team competition in which we competed, from Open through 70+ for the men and from Open through 60+ for the women.  At the national level, our teams stood atop the podium six different times at USATF Masters National races.  What's more, five of our teams made the podium in the 2024 USATF Masters National Grand Prix: 50+ women (2nd), 60+ women (1st), 40+ men (2nd), 50+ men (2nd) and 60+ men (2nd).

Read on for a summary of our magnificent team and individual results.
