Shore AC Blossoms at Spring Classic 15K

Our bold harriers in white wasted no time shattering the freezing clutches of winter from their limbs on the first day of spring.  They burst forth at the Spring Classic 15K race and toasted the competition.​  Shore Athletic Club won the team competition in nearly every category.

Thanks to the outstanding team performances, Shore AC jumped out to the early lead in the Club Grand Prix competition.  Unofficially, club standings after one race are as follows.
