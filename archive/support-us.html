​Become a Partner

Become a sponsor or partner of the Shore Athletic Club. There are several opportunities available for us to work together as we strive to support our hard working and dedicated community of runners and track & field athletes. From each individual's efforts, teams form, comradery builds, goals get accomplished and memories are made. Let's collaborate to help continue this vibrant cycle. 
The Robinson Ale House in Asbury Park is a long standing partner of the Shore A.C. offering space, complimentary offerings to our participants after a few of our signature events and more! For inquiries to partner with The Shore Athletic Club please send us an email at [email protected].

Become a Member or Renew

Join Shore A.C. and support the club that supports you! Anyone can join Shore A.C. There are no age or ability limitations and no commitment or performance expectations. Many new benefits for those who pay their membership dues! 

You can be a member and participate in Shore A.C. hosted events and/or be a member and compete for Shore A.C. in USATF Grand Prix and Championship Events. All new members get a one-time racing singlet and an automatic discount to 5 Shore A.C. hosted events including the  Captain Zinn Memorial Races, Big Bang Mile, Sheehan Classic 5K, Jersey Shore Half Marathon  and the Polar Bear Races.​

Become a Volunteer​

Want to join a solid community? Interested in meeting fun and friendly people? Need Hours?
Shore A.C. has the perfect opportunities for you! We host abundance of events per year and need volunteers for all of them. We are building committees so if you're interested in supporting the planning process and/or on event day, let us know! Please reach out to learn more. We are happy to write volunteer confirmation letters and sign off on volunteer hours.
