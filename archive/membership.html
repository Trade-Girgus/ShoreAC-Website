Description

At the Shore Athletic Club, we are passionate about nurturing athletes of all ages and skill levels! Our mission is to inspire and develop junior and youth runners, as well as men’s and women’s open and masters track and field athletes, distance and cross country runners, and racewalkers. We believe in the power of competition to ignite the human spirit and aim to promote track and field as a lifelong passion for everyone. 

We proudly host a variety of events for boys and girls, youth and juniors, and both men and women, offering exciting opportunities in competitive track and field, distance running, cross country, and racewalking across the Jersey Shore, New Jersey, and beyond. 

With your support, we’re enthusiastic about continuing to enhance our offerings and making an even greater impact in the year ahead. Let’s make it our best year yet together!

Youth Membership

Gone Running

Gone Running offers programs in Manalapan, Holmdel, Middletown, Manasquan, and South Burlington, with plans to expand youth opportunities in 2026. The goal of Gone Running is to provide positive experiences through running and fitness training that:

1. Encourage a vibrant and healthy lifestyle.
2. Boost your child's confidence and enhance their performance in any sport they choose to participate in, such as soccer, football, basketball, or baseball.
3. Cater to your child's current ability level, whether they are a beginner, intermediate, or advanced athlete.

When fitness is enjoyable, children achieve the best, most consistent, and lasting results. By making physical activity fun, children are more likely to incorporate it into their lives as adults. We take pride in teaching safe and effective running techniques, including drills that benefit your child in any sport they wish to pursue.

KIDS' Runs at Shore A.C. Events

Take advantage of these community events that are available to you! There are youth races at our Captain Zinn Memorial 5K in April, the Elliott Denman NJ International Meet in June, the Sheehan Classic in August, the Big Bang Mile in July, the Jersey Shore Half Marathon in September, and the Polar Bear Races in December. In addition, we host a Summer All-Comers Track Series, a 1500M kids run at our Lake Takanassee Summer series on Monday nights in July and August, and a 6-week Lester Wright Jr. Youth XC series from September to November.
