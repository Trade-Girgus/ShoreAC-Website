​​Shore AC Reaches New Heights in 2023

December 9, 2023, Tallahassee, Florida: Suzanne La Burt (left) and Nora Cary (right) unfurl the banner that commemorates first place for Shore Athletic Club's women's 40+ team in the 2023 USATF Masters National Long Distance Running Grand Prix.
It goes to show that we love running -- fast!  Shore Athletic Club compiled a tremendous resume in 2023.  At the state (a.k.a. the USATF Association) level, we won every LDR Grand Prix team competition in which we competed, from Open through 70+ for the men and from Open through 60+ for the women.  At the national level, our teams stood atop the podium six different times at USATF Masters National races.  What's more, four of our teams made the podium in the 2023 USATF Masters National Grand Prix: 40+ women (1st), 60+ women (tied for 2nd), 50+ men (3rd) and 60+ men (2nd).

Read on for a summary of our magnificent team and individual results.
