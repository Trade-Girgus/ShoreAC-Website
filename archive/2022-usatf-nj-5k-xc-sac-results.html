Shore AC Extends GP Leads With Strong Showing at 5K XC Race

Every year, the USATF New Jersey Association includes two cross country races in the Long Distance Running Grand Prix.  One is a 5-kilometer tilt.  The other goes 8 kilometers (though this year's version will be 6K for women).  In both cases, USATF-NJ awards 2 additional Grand Prix points per team as an incentive to participate in the cross country races.

Shore AC took advantage of this opportunity to pile on more GP points by deploying 12 teams in today's USATF-NJ 5K XC race.  The teams performed well, too!  On the men's side, Shore AC teams took first place in every age group by 70+.  On the women's side -- where we had only masters-age runners, and all but two of age 59 or older -- we won in 40+ and 50+ and took second in Open and 60+.

As a result of our good fortune, Shore AC stacked more points onto our league-leading total in the 2022 USATF-NJ Paull Miller Auto Group Long Distance Running Club Grand Prix.  Unofficially, we now have 510 club points.  Clifton occupies second place with 234 points, while Fleet Feet / Essex lurks close behind in third place with 221 points.  Meanwhile, Raritan Valley is knocking on the door of opportunity in fourth place with 207 points.

In team competition, Shore AC holds a sizeable lead for nearly every age group.  Here are the unofficial numbers.
