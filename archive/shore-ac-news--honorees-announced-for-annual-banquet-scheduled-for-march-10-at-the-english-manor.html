HONOREES ANNOUNCED FOR ANNUAL BANQUET SCHEDULED FOR MARCH 10 AT THE ENGLISH MANOR

2/19/2019

1 Comment

 
High-level achievers and major-meet medalists in all categories highlight the
Honors List of awardees who will be saluted at the annual Shore Athletic Club
Awards Banquet scheduled for March 10 at The English Manor
catering facility, Ocean Township, NJ.

   Michael Kish, double medalist in the M65-division sprints at the
World Masters Championships in Malaga, Spain, has been named winner
of the Johnny Hayes Memorial Award as the club's athlete of the year.

 Newest members of the Shore AC Hall of Fame will be Tony Plaster,
John Soucheck, Larry Scheid and Mike Geraltowski.
