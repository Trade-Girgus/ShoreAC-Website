63rd ANNUAL CAPTAIN ZINN MEMORIAL RACES 
Event Date: Sunday, April 12, 2026
Event Location: Dorbrook Park - 353 County Road 537, Colts Neck, NJ 07722
Event Details: This event honors all Vietnam Veterans and is named in memory of Captain Ronald Lloyd "Ron" Zinn, a race walker from the United States who was killed in Vietnam in 1965. He graduated from the US Military Academy in 1962 and represented the USA at both the 1960 (Rome) and 1964 (Tokyo) Summer Olympics. He placed 6th in the 20K race walk in 1964. Shore AC has held this race to remember Captain Zinn since 1966. The day features a 5k Run at 10:00am and Race Walk at 10:05am, along with a kids fun run at 9:30AM. Sponsorship opportunities are also available. More details on the Run Signup website linked below. First 200 people to register receive an event tee.

Contact: Erin O'Neill

ALL COMERS TRACK SERIES 
Event Dates: Saturday, June 6, 13, and 20
Event Location: Ranney School - 235 Hope Road Tinton Falls, N.J. 
Event Details: Each scheduled Saturday, Shore A.C. hosts a track meet open to athletes of all ages and abilities. In addition to offering jumping and throwing events, each meet holds at least one track race in each of the following categories: sprint, middle-distance, long-distance, race-walk, and relay. These community-based meets traditionally attract hundreds of athletes of all ages, including young girls and boys, high school and college students, post-collegiate athletes, and Masters Competitors up to their 70s and 80s. Pre-registration required. Meets begins at 9:00 AM and the order of events are to be determined.

JERSEY SHORE HALF MARATHON AND 2 MILE RACE 
New start time: 8:00 AM
Event Date: Sunday, October 4, 2026
Event Location: Gateway National Recreation Area, Sandy Hook, New Jersey (Lot D)
Event Details: Beginning on January 9, 1972, the extensive history of the Jersey Shore Half Marathon places it as one of the oldest and most prestigious distance events in New Jersey. Whether preparing for a full marathon or enjoying the gift of fitness, runners are sure to cruise on this flat, fast course through some of the most iconic areas of the Jersey Shore. Additionally, the Jersey Shore Half Marathon offers official pacers, mile split times, race clocks through-out the course, water stations, and complimentary race t-shirts and medals. Once participants cross the finish line, they can head right on over to the post-race party to celebrate at the Lifesaving Station between lots D and E.

63rd ANNUAL ASBURY PARK POLAR BEAR RACES 
Event Date: Sunday, December 27, 2026
Event Location: Convention Hall – 1300 Ocean Avenue Asbury Park, NJ 07712
Event Details: Founded 61 years ago by Shore A.C., Polar Bear, the club's longest-standing and most legendary race, has never been rescheduled due to inclement weather. Even after dealing with the devastating effects left by Superstorm Sandy, Shore A.C. organizers proudly kept the annual event up and running. Rest assured, the job always gets done! Rain, snow, or sunshine, the 1 mile race begins at 9:30am. There will be 1 start time to run the 5k challenge at 10:00am. Results will be cumulative. Awards will be given to the overall top 3 male and female finishers and the top male and female finishers in each age group. 
REGISTRATION
Contact: Dayna Luma
