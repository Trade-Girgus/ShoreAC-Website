"GORDON’S JUNIOR OLYMPICS WIN EARNS HER USATF ATHLETE OF THE WEEK HONORS" - USATF

1/2/2022

0 Comments

 
"INDIANAPOLIS — Winning the 15-18 girls’ race at the USATF Junior Olympics, Liliah Gordon (Columbus, New Jersey) earned recognition as the 37th USATF Athlete of the Week award winner for 2021. 

Representing Shore Athletic Club at the USATF National Junior Olympic Cross Country Championships in Paris, Kentucky, Gordon overcame unfriendly weather conditions to dominate the 15-18 age division 5K race in 19:21.6, almost 24 seconds ahead of the next finisher in the 129-athlete race. That performance earned Gordon the age 15-16 title."
