TRAINING SESSIONS
Join us for a professional approach to running and track & field training on Wednesday evenings at Mater Dei Track. This comprehensive workout program, designed for adults and youth of all skill levels, includes warm-up techniques, dynamic drills, endurance and speed training, strength training, flexibility, and mindfulness! The adult and youth groups will be separate, ensuring a focused and effective training environment. This program is for beginners, intermediate, and advanced runners. 

Let’s keep the momentum alive, whether it's between seasons or during them! Join us every Wednesday at the Mater Dei Track in Middletown, where the Shore A.C. community unites for an energizing training experience. We offer coaching in distance, mid-distance, sprints, shot put, long jump, and high jump.

All adult club members receive free workouts. You don't need to register. Youth dates and prices are listed below.

MEET THE COACHES
Coach Joe Compagni will run the adult sessions. Coach Joe comes to Shore A.C. and Gone Running after 24 years of experience as the Director and Head Coach of Men’s and Women’s Track & Field and Cross Country at Monmouth University. Under his leadership, they won a total of 58 team championships in both the NEC and MAAC. Compagni earned Coach of the Year honors on 58 different occasions - in the Northeast Conference (NEC), the Metro Atlantic Athletic Conference (MAAC), and eight times as a USTFCCCA NCAA Division I Mid-Atlantic Region Coach of the Year. Coach Joe also serves as the Vice President of Shore A.C. Erin O'Neill will lead the youth sessions. Coach Erin is a USA Track & Field-certified coach, a state-certified elementary and special education teacher, the President of Shore A.C., an event director, and a competitor. Erin has been with the Shore A.C. for eight years and with Gone Running for five years.

The Shore Athletic Club

The Shore Athletic Club (Shore AC) was officially established in 1934. The club's early years in the 1930s and 1940s were marked by remarkable achievements. After a revival in the late 1940s, the club continued its journey into the early 1950s. In 1964, a new era began for Shore AC, and we have since accomplished many great things. Our membership has grown to over 500 individuals.

In addition to competing in major events, Shore AC organizes a fantastic variety of events for everyone to participate in and enjoy. We host four signature events each year: the NJ International Track & Field Meet, the Big Bang Mile, the Asbury Park Sheehan Classic, and the Jersey Shore Half Marathon. Furthermore, Shore AC is dedicated to training athletes to compete in certified youth, open, and masters USATF events, as well as long-distance races, both nationally and internationally throughout the year. 

We maintain a strong, competitive presence at prestigious events such as the Millrose Games, the Penn Relays, and various USATF National and State Championship events. Notably, Shore AC proudly sent several athletes to the 2021 Olympic Trials!

The club's Mission Statement is “to develop junior and youth athletes, as well as men’s and women’s open and masters track and field athletes, distance and cross country runners, and racewalkers. We strive to activate the human spirit by providing competitive events for all ages and groups, and to promote the entire sport in all its forms as a lifelong activity.”
