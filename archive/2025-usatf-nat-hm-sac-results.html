Shore AC Well Represented by Siriano at USATF Masters National Half Marathon

The second race of the 2026 USATF Masters National Long Distance Running Grand Prix was held in Spicewood, Texas.  Because of calendar constraints plus a disinclination for running an 8-lap half marathon, only one Shore AC member showed an interest in this race.  That member was 59-year-old Scott Siriano.  He survived the mind-numbing loopy course to run a very solid time of 1:29:10.  That earned Scott second place in his 55-59 age group.  He thereby garners 95 points in the 2026 Individual Grand Prix.  Bravo, Scott!!!
