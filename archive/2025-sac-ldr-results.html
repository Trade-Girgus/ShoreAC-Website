​​Shore AC Long Distance Runners Thrive in 2025

This was another superlative year for Shore Athletic Club.  We amassed team wins and exalted in a cornucopia of outstanding individual results.  At the USATF New Jersey Association level, for the third year in a row, we won every LDR Grand Prix team competition in which we competed (well, all but one), from Open through 80+ for the men and from Open through 60+ for the women.  At the national level, our teams snared the top podium position a whopping nine times at USATF Masters National races.  In addition, four of our teams made the podium in the 2025 USATF Masters National Grand Prix: 60+ women (1st), 50+ men (2nd), 70+ men (3rd), and 80+ men (1st).

Read on for a summary of our magnificent team and individual results.
