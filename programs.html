Youth programs

The club is built as an inclusive track and field home for athletes across event groups and ability levels.

What youth athletes can try

  • Sprints, hurdles, middle distance, distance, and cross country
  • Jumps, throws, multi-events, relays, and racewalking
  • Road running from youth fun runs through longer racing as athletes mature

Race opportunities

Youth athletes can race at Captain Zinn, All-Comers, Lake Takanassee, Big Bang Mile, Youth XC, Bill Bruno Alumni XC, and Polar Bear events.

Youth race calendar

Why it works

Track and field is a lifetime sport. Shore A.C. lets young athletes find their event while learning around experienced coaches, volunteers, and older competitors.

Gone Running youth training

Gone Running offers 10 to 14 week youth running and fitness programs with positive coaching, drills, form work, and preparation for Junior Olympic competition.

Locations

  • Chesterfield
  • Manalapan
  • Marlboro
  • Manasquan
  • Middletown
  • Holmdel

Program focus

The program emphasizes fitness, confidence, safe progression, and a good first experience with running. It also gives motivated athletes a pathway toward Junior Olympic cross country and track and field.
