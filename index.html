Why join Shore A.C.?

Members get more than a club name. They get race discounts, training access, competition opportunities, coaching paths, and community.

  • One-time Shore A.C. racing singlet for new members
  • $10 discount at five hosted events
  • Free adult track workouts and youth workout discounts
  • Club communications, incentives, and group chat
  • USATF competition opportunities and group runs
  • Optional individualized coaching plans and flexible sessions

Gone Running

10-14 week youth running and fitness programs built around positive experiences, confidence, form, drills, and preparation for Junior Olympic competition.

Locations include Chesterfield, Manalapan, Marlboro, Manasquan, Middletown, and Holmdel.
