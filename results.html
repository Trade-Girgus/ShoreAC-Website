MOPL

The Monmouth-Ocean Parochial League resources on the original site are document-heavy. A full redesign would be worthwhile if MOPL is a current priority.

What should be redesigned

Invoices, rules, coach contact lists, orders of events, and championship materials should become a clean local document library if MOPL is still active.

Why not copy everything into race cards

Those documents serve coaches and administrators, not casual race registrants. Keeping them in a resources hub is easier to navigate.

Recommended next step

Create a MOPL page with current-year PDF cards, archive filters, and contact routing once the club confirms which documents are current.
