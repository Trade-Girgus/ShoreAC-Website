Become a sponsor or partner

Business and community partners can support race experiences, awards, athlete development, youth programs, and club operations.

Why partner

The club serves dedicated runners, track and field athletes, racewalkers, families, volunteers, and community members across the Jersey Shore. Partnerships can connect your organization with a loyal local endurance and athletics audience.

Start the conversation

Sponsorship questions can be sent to the club leadership team for follow-up and current partnership options.
